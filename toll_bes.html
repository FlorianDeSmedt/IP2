Bayesian equilibration: two roads, two toll authorities

How to read the charts

  • Heatmaps. Each player's GP estimate of its revenue (darker = higher). Dots: model runs so far. Red circles: this iteration's check runs.
  • Best-response curves. Blue: player 1's best τ₁ for each τ₂. Orange: player 2's best τ₂ for each τ₁. Solid = from the GPs, dashed = true (BES never sees these).
  • Equilibrium is where the curves cross: no player wants to change its toll. Red diamond = BES candidate, green star = exact answer. Over the iterations the solid curves move onto the dashed ones.
  • Roads. Traffic at the candidate tolls (thicker = more vehicles). A cheaper road draws traffic from the other.
  • Nudge gains. The most each player wins by moving its own toll ±δ in the real model. Both below ε (dotted) = ε-local Nash equilibrium: BES stops. The distance to the exact Nash is only known because this toy model can be solved exactly.
  • Seed. BES starts from N_INIT random toll pairs (open squares); SEED decides where. Everything after follows from them, so another seed gives a different path, cost and answer. This run may be lucky: set N_SEEDS to 50 to see the spread.
