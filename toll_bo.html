Bayesian Optimisation of a single toll

How to read the charts

  • Top chart. The dashed line is the true revenue, which the algorithm never sees. The blue line is the GP's best guess, and the shaded band is its uncertainty (±2σ): narrow near the evaluations, wide far from them.
  • Red numbered dots are the runs of the expensive model, in order. The green dotted line is the toll the algorithm will try next.
  • Middle chart. Expected Improvement: how much each toll is expected to beat the best revenue so far. Its peak is the next toll to try. EI is high where the GP predicts a high revenue (exploitation) or is very unsure (exploration); the title splits it into these two parts.
  • Bottom chart. How far each run is from the true best revenue R*, on a log scale. The line is the best result so far.
  • Converged when EI is close to zero everywhere and the algorithm picks (almost) the same toll again: there is nothing left to learn.

Try a small LENGTH (a wigglier GP that explores more) or a large XI (more exploration), then press Run.
